# CSCE1710-Project-1-Summer-2025
A project I created for my Summer 2025 Computer Science 1 Class, implementing switches and do-while loops.
This project uses imaginary job positions, allowing a user to enter their name, hours worked per week, and job title to generate how much money they make a month. 

To start, you must enter a first and last name containing alphabet letters and spaces only. A space must be included, and an error message will show for any invalid names.
EX:
John(Invalid)
John (Invalid)
John 123(Invalid)
John Smith(Valid)

You will then be prompted to enter the number of hours you work per week. The number may only be an integer or decimal number.

You will then be prompted to enter a number representing a job title. Any number not associated with a job title will result in invalid and you will be prompted to enter again until an acceptable number is entered.

After that, the code will take into account the number of hours worked and job title, and apply all associated bonuses to give the user their salary in a month.

In the end, you may choose to enter another employee or close the program.
